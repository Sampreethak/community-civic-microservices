Why the DEPT-[A-Z]{3}-\d{3} ID Pattern Was Chosen

1. Semantic Readability

DEPT-WAT-101 immediately identifies Water Supply Division 101 to operators and citizens without raw integer ambiguity.

2. Zero-DB Gateway Routing

API Gateway can parse sector tokens (WAT, ELE) using regex to route tickets to specific microservice queues with zero database queries.

3. Multi-Zone Collision Resistance

Namespaced keys prevent primary-key collisions during municipal amalgamations or state-level federation.

4. NUDM & ISO Alignment

Conforms to India's National Urban Digital Mission (NUDM) taxonomy and ISO 3166-2 public administration standards.
